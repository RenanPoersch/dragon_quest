# Prompts — pixel art, parte 10A

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, em 12 de outubro de 2026. Imagens originais para esta demo; FF6 original foi a referência visual, sem importação de assets do jogo. Bram e moradores tiveram uma revisão de espaçamento antes da integração. Os PNGs originais foram preservados; recortes de sprites são feitos em memória pela Unity.

## aren

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite sheet for a Unity 2.5D fantasy RPG. Create ORIGINAL character Aren, a young adventurer, short dark brown hair, ochre/tan tunic, leather boots and belt, muted rust cloak, a small sheathed sword. Visual language inspired by the ORIGINAL 16-bit Super Nintendo Final Fantasy VI: tiny expressive chibi pixel sprite, dark selective outline, compact pixel clusters, restrained warm colors, hand-drawn pixel shadows, not a smooth cartoon or rendered 3D figure. Transparent background, no floor, no cast shadow. CRITICAL LAYOUT: exactly 1024x768 pixels, a perfectly regular 4-column by 3-row sheet of 256x256 cells, no grid lines. Every cell has exactly ONE complete full-body sprite, entirely inside the cell. Columns left to right: facing DOWN toward viewer, facing LEFT in profile, facing RIGHT in profile, facing UP showing back. Rows top to bottom: idle with feet together, walking with left foot forward, walking with right foot forward. Twelve poses TOTAL of the SAME character, SAME clothes, SAME head/body size and SAME foot baseline in every cell. Character silhouette uses a logical 32x32 pixel cell, enlarged with nearest-neighbor pixels by 8x; occupies roughly 20 logical pixels width and 27 logical pixels height, centered horizontally, feet at logical y=29. Render exact crisp square pixels, no antialiasing, no gradients, no blur. Side poses must read as walking profile rather than three quarter view. Up column must show back of head and cloak. No lettering, captions, cell frames, logos or watermarks; do not copy any existing Final Fantasy character.

## terrain

Exactly 4 columns by 3 rows of uniform SQUARE material swatches, full image 1024x768 desired. Every swatch fills its cell EDGE TO EDGE and must itself be seamless when repeated; no gutters, margins, labels, grid dividers, lighting gradient, perspective, objects, or scene. Opaque background, no text, logos, watermark.

## bramV2

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target is the supplied original transparent sprite sheet. Change ONLY the layout/padding of its existing twelve sprites. Preserve every character identity, clothes, faces, pixel-art style, colors and poses; add no characters. Keep the exact overall image aspect ratio 4:3 and the PERFECT uniform FOUR COLUMN, THREE ROW cell grid. The sprites in the source straddle row boundaries. FIX this: reduce each complete sprite uniformly by about 20% and place it fully INSIDE its own cell with at least 10% empty TRANSPARENT margin on EVERY side. All twelve sprites should have the same consistent foot baseline 85% down their cell, not at the cell boundary. Each cell contains precisely one complete full body. Row boundaries at EXACT one-third and two-thirds of image height; column boundaries at exact quarters. No part of any head, hair, weapon, cloak or foot may touch/cross a cell boundary. No cropping of sprites. Keep nearest-neighbor crisp pixel blocks and true transparent alpha; no antialiasing, shadows, grid lines, backgrounds, frames, labels or text. This is a technical sprite-sheet layout repair, not a redesign.
